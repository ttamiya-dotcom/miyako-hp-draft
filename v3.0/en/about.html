Investment strategySTRATEGY

From academic insight to the industries of tomorrow.

MIYAKO Capital is an independent, private venture capital firm. We invest in technology-driven startups that put cutting-edge intellectual assets from Kyoto University and other academic and research institutions to work. Through growth support such as industry-academia collaboration that brings new technologies into society, we aim to help create the leading industries and innovations of the next generation.

Our approachAPPROACH

01

ResearchResearch

Cutting-edge intellectual assets from Kyoto University and other academic and research institutions

  • Life sciences and biotechnology
  • IT and electronics (including AI and IoT)
  • Agriculture and food
  • Environment and energy
  • Chemicals and materials

and a wide range of other technologies and industries

02

InvestmentInvestment

All stages, with a focus on seed and early stage, including middle and later stage

Businesses and teams from Japan that aim to expand overseas and have the potential to succeed in global markets

03

Growth supportSupport

  • Industry-academia collaborationEven if a company is not using research or technology from Kyoto University or other institutions when we first invest, we work with it after investment to put effective industry-academia collaboration in place that fits its situation and needs, and we keep supporting it over time.
  • Global expansionFrom our Silicon Valley office, we plan and carry out strategies for reaching global markets.
  • Hands-on supportEvery member of our team has extensive experience in venture investing in Japan, the US and other markets, as well as in founding and running startups. Building on this, we provide thorough, practical hands-on support.
