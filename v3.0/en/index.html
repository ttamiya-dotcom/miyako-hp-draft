Investment strategySTRATEGY

From academic insight to the industries of tomorrow.

MIYAKO Capital is an independent, private venture capital firm. We invest in technology-driven startups that put cutting-edge intellectual assets from Kyoto University and other academic and research institutions to work. Through growth support such as industry-academia collaboration that brings new technologies into society, we aim to help create the leading industries and innovations of the next generation.

SUPPORT / 01

Industry-academia collaboration

Even if a company is not using research or technology from Kyoto University or other institutions when we first invest, we work with it after investment to put effective industry-academia collaboration in place that fits its situation and needs, and we keep supporting it over time.

SUPPORT / 02

Global expansion

From our Silicon Valley office, we plan and carry out strategies for reaching global markets.

SUPPORT / 03

Hands-on support

Every member of our team has extensive experience in venture investing in Japan, the US and other markets, as well as in founding and running startups. Building on this, we provide thorough, practical hands-on support.
