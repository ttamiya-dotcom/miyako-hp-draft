Partner

Hiroyuki Misawa

Hiroyuki has been with the firm since its founding, leading investments in life science and biotech. He brings deep expertise across drug discovery and medical devices, supporting academia-originated startups from early-stage commercialization through to exit.

  • Prior to joining, he spent seven years as an academic researcher and educator in the life sciences.
  • In 2002, he joined the founding team of an incubation fund specializing in regenerative medicine and medical devices at a subsidiary of Japan Asia Investment Co. (JAIC), where he managed fund formation, deal sourcing, and investment execution.
  • Following his transfer to JAIC in 2007, he served as investment director and fund manager for QOL-related sectors, building one of Japan's most recognized track records across regenerative medicine, drug discovery, medical devices, and health services.
  • He is a recipient of the Japan Venture Awards (JVA) 2020 Venture Capitalist Encouragement Award.

Ph.D., Saitama University

Partner, Founder

Satoshi Yamaguchi

Satoshi founded the firm and leads its investment strategy, deal origination, and portfolio company development. Leveraging strong relationships with universities and public institutions, he drives the commercialization of academia-originated startups.

  • He brings over 30 years of experience in venture capital and SME management.
  • Joining JAFCO in 1988, he rose to Investment Director and Head of Investment, overseeing the formation and management of funds totaling over ¥100 billion, and leading investments in more than 100 ventures across Japan, the US, and China, which generated significant returns through IPOs and strategic partnerships.
  • Following JAFCO, he served in executive roles at a Japan-China media and distribution conglomerate, while advising numerous ventures as a board member and executing multiple listed-company M&A and secondary financing transactions.

B.A. in Economics, Yokohama National University

Partner

Tsunesaburo Sugaya

Tsunesaburo has been a partner at the firm since its early days, serving as the firm's gateway to the US and global markets through his Silicon Valley base. He leverages decades of American VC experience and an extensive international network to support portfolio companies in their global expansion.

  • He joined JAFCO in 1999 and, as a General Partner, played a central role in establishing and managing six US funds totaling approximately ¥140 billion, delivering strong performance throughout.
  • From 2003, as CEO of JAFCO USA (now Icon Ventures), he rebuilt the US operation, which subsequently ranked among the top 25 VC firms nationwide (AlwaysOn, 2013).
  • He was also individually recognized as one of the Top 100 Venture Partners globally (AlwaysOn, 2013), the first Japanese national to receive this distinction.

B.S. in Science and Technology, National Defense Academy of JapanVisiting Professor, Yamagata UniversityCalifornia Fellow, Kyushu University

Partner

He Rui

Rui joined the firm at the establishment of MIYAKO Kyoto University Innovation II L.P., where he oversees the full investment cycle from deal sourcing through to exit, with a track record that includes both IPOs and trade sales.

  • Prior to joining, he held technical roles at a global manufacturer and business development positions at an environment and energy consultancy.

His experience there includes a capital and business alliance between a Chinese unicorn (later listed) and a Japanese public company, as well as a cross-border M&A transaction, giving him broad expertise across deal sourcing, business due diligence, and post-investment value creation.
